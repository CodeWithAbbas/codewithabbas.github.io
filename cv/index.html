Professional Summary

PhD researcher at the intersection of FPGA design and hardware security for cyber-physical systems, developing hardware fingerprinting for physical-layer device authentication that identifies embedded devices from the analog imperfections in their digital switching signals. Validated on production automotive ECUs and extending to drones and robotic platforms. Two years of industry experience in FPGA and RTL design before the PhD.
