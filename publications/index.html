    Short: Is Your ECU Really Yours?
    Abbas Ali and Hafiz Malik
    In 4th USENIX Symposium on Vehicle Security and Privacy (VehicleSec 26), Aug 2026
    Awarded Abs Bib HTML PDF

    Artifact Evaluated: Available, Functional, Reproduced

    A hardware fingerprinting framework for authenticating embedded devices in vehicular networks. The approach analyses the analog characteristics of digital switching behaviour to detect compromised or counterfeit devices, extracting 211 waveform features and applying Random Forest and stacked ensemble methods to achieve 99.5% accuracy under controlled conditions and 100% device type classification across the tested hardware.
